Point of view

Most automation saves labour.
The interesting kind changes what a team can attempt.

We treat artificial intelligence not as an ornament on an existing process, but as a rethinking of the work itself. When routine cognition becomes abundant, the role of human judgment does not diminish — it becomes the limiting factor.

We design systems that work beside people rather than in front of them: legible, steerable, and honest about what they can and cannot do.

Automation compounds. Every system we ship is a step toward intelligence that can plan, reason and act over long horizons — what many now call superintelligence. We think that destination deserves sober engineering, not slogans.
